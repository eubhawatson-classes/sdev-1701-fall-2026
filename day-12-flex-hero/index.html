Gie yer feet a road, an the Heilans'll gie ye a bield.

Forty-twa bothies strung athort the Heilans, fae Glen Affric tae the Flow Country. Nae bookin fees, nae crowds, jist a dry roof, a peat fire an a view that'll hae ye greetin intae yer porridge. The nichts are drawin in, sae plan yer stravaig afore the snaw does it for ye.
