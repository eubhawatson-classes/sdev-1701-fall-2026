Slow Cooker Beef Stew

Content borrowed from: thecozycook.com

Ingredients

2 lbs beef stew meat, cut into 1" cubes ¼ cup all-purpose flour ½ tsp salt ½ tsp ground black pepper 1 clove garlic, minced 1 bay leaf 1 tsp paprika 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce 1 onion, chopped 1½ cups beef broth 3 potatoes, diced 4 carrots, sliced 1 stalk celery, chopped Directions [Image inside the figure] Caption: Optional ingredient additions include green beans, parsnips, and sweet potatoes. Place meat in slow cooker; add garlic, bay leaf, paprika, Worcestershire sauce, onion, broth, potatoes, carrots, and celery. In a small bowl, mix flour, salt, and pepper; sprinkle over meat and stir to coat. Cover and cook on Low for 10–12 hours or on High for 4–6 hours. Remove bay leaf, stir gently, and serve hot. Ingredient Terms Beef Stew Meat Well-marbled beef trimmed and cut into 1-inch cubes for even cooking. Worcestershire Sauce A fermented liquid condiment that adds umami and depth of flavor. Bay Leaf Leaf of the bay laurel tree; infuses a subtle herbal aroma. Notes Easy Cleanup: Use a slow cooker liner for fuss-free cleanup.
