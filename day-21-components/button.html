Buttons

A button has to stand out enough to look clickable, and it has to react when you use it. Each reaction is called a state.

States

  • Hover: move the cursor on top of it with your mouse.
  • Focus: press Tab until the button gets an orange ring.
  • Active: click and hold.

Links vs buttons

Use <a> when it goes somewhere, like another page. Use <button> when it performs a behaviour, like sending a form or saving. The .button class makes both look the same.

They don't behave the same, though. Try right-clicking each one: the link offers to open in a new tab or copy its address, and the button doesn't, because it isn't going anywhere.
