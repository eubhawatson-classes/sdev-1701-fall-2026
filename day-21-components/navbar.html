Navbar

At the top of this page is a classic navbar. It is used for top-level (i.e., high-level) navigation.

It's usually laid out horizontally, but may be hidden behind a hamburger menu. People expect the brand on the left, the main links in a row, and the same bar in the same spot on every page.

Hover over the links, then Tab through them. The current page is marked, so you always know where you are.
