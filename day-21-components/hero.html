Hero banner

A hero banner is the big section at the top of a page. It has one job: tell people where they are and what to do next, in a few seconds.

A hero banner should have one message. The heading should say with the site is about in a few words.

There should be one main action. We are giving it some emphasis with the button style from earlier. The secondary action is still there for easy access, but it is given a plainer style to visually enforce its level of relative importance.
